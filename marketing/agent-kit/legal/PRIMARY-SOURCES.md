## Implemented license correction

The kit LICENSE's former unconditional warranty exclusion was replaced with:

> Nothing in this license excludes, restricts or modifies any guarantee, warranty,
> condition, right or remedy that cannot lawfully be excluded, restricted or
> modified, including under the Australian Consumer Law and applicable consumer
> laws in other jurisdictions. Any statutory rights to remedies for defective
> digital content or to required updates remain unaffected. Subject to those
> non-excludable rights and any express commitments in the applicable sales terms,
> the software is provided "as is", without additional warranties.

This preserves mandatory rights under ACL s64 and applicable consumer digital
rules, without adding an artificial liability cap or changing the existing grant.
It does not certify all remaining sales terms, tax arrangements or worldwide rules.
Verify that the final sold artifact includes the correction and that all checkout
terms preserve it. Earlier valid public grants and third-party rights remain.
