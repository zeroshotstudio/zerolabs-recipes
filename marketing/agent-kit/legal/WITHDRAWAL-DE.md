## Implementation notes — keep outside the published notice

1. These paragraphs follow the current statutory model and select the
   contract-conclusion start for downloads, not the delivery-date start for
   physical goods. Do not add physical return conditions or a return-shipping fee.
2. The unwaived approach permits immediate download while retaining withdrawal.
   Do not insert mandatory early-expiry consent, a preselected waiver, “no refund
   after download”, deductions for use or a condition requiring reasons.
3. Ordinary withdrawal refund is prompt and no later than 14 days after receipt.
   The online-function receipt is immediate and must include the declaration and
   date/time. They are separate obligations; a “refund processed” notice days
   later is not the required immediate receipt.
4. Wrong or missing notice can extend the withdrawal period. Do not claim the
   ordinary 14-day limit regardless of notice compliance.
5. German law does not impose compensation for use of downloaded digital content
   on withdrawal (§ 357a(3) BGB). Cessation of use under § 357(8) is not a basis for
   withholding/delaying the refund pending a deletion certificate.
6. The statutory form is optional for the consumer. A clear email or other
   legally effective declaration must also be accepted. Localize the notice/form
   for other served countries as required and implement their mandatory rights.

Official models: [EGBGB Anlage 1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgbeg/art_253anlage_1.html?data=false)
and [EGBGB Anlage 2](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/bgbeg/art_253anlage_2.html).
