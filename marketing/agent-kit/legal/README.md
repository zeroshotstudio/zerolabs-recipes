Each bracketed `[REQUIRED: ...]` item is unresolved. They are intentional release
blockers, not customer text. In particular, a checkout provider is not yet chosen,
and a payment processor must not be described as a merchant of record without
contractual evidence. Select the correct seller branch before publishing.

The proposed EU consumer approach keeps the statutory withdrawal right intact
even after an immediate download. It does **not** request an early-expiry waiver.
It requires a functioning online withdrawal flow under current German § 356a BGB,
in addition to email/postal withdrawal. Australia has no general statutory
change-of-mind period for an ordinary online digital sale; no voluntary worldwide
14-day promise is assumed or approved by these drafts.

The kit's unqualified warranty disclaimer has been corrected in the repository
with a universal mandatory-rights carveout, preserving the existing license
grant and restrictions. These sales drafts must not undo that protection or any
earlier valid MIT grants. Prior public releases and third-party licenses retain
their existing rights.
